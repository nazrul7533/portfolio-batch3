About Me

A career built on trust, control & clarity.

I hold BBA and MBA degrees from the University of Dhaka. I began my professional journey as a Customer Service Manager at Grameenphone Ltd., where I learned the value of putting customers first.

In 2008, I joined Mercantile Bank PLC as a Probationary Officer. Over 18 years I have worked across the Credit Administration Division, the Public Relations Division and, today, the Branches Operations Control Division, which I now lead as Divisional Head.

My current focus is policy guideline formulation: turning regulatory requirements and operational experience into clear, practical policies that branches can follow with confidence.
